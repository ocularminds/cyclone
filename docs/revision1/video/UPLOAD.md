**Title:** Cyclone — hands-first review of AI actions

**Description:**

Cyclone puts held AI actions on a miniature factory at your desk. Inspect a proposal, scrub its kinematic
rehearsal, amend the value, and decide with your hands. Every demo decision retains a signed local receipt
and the model evidence that was reviewed.

This is actual Immersive Web Emulator footage, edited from two hand-driven takes at normal speed. The
end card is the application's capture scene. Inputs are emulated; signatures use a local demo key;
execution is simulated. No real machine is connected. A rehearsal is not a prediction or guarantee,
and a signed record is not proof of physical execution.
