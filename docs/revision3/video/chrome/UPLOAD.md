## Title

Cyclone — hands-first review of AI actions (HD demo)

## Description

Cyclone puts held AI actions on a miniature factory at your desk. Inspect a proposal, scrub its kinematic rehearsal, amend the value, and decide with your hands. Every demo decision retains a signed local receipt and the model evidence that was reviewed.

This is actual Immersive Web Emulator footage recorded in external Chrome with the Meta Quest 3 emulator, edited from hand-driven takes at normal speed. Captured natively at 3840×2160 and delivered at 1080p. The end card is the application's capture scene. Inputs are emulated; signatures use a local demo key; execution is simulated. No real machine is connected. A rehearsal is not a prediction or guarantee, and a signed record is not proof of physical execution.

Try the demo: https://ocularminds.github.io/cyclone/
